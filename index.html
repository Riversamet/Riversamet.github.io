QTRAP — automated QM/MM dynamics

A workflow that automates quasiclassical QM/MM dynamics simulations end to end: a ten-step pipeline turning a raw molecular dynamics trajectory into completed runs on an HPC cluster, plus an agentic layer that lets an AI agent drive the whole procedure.

  • No automated workflow for quasiclassical QM/MM dynamics had been published before this one.
  • Covers the full path from NetCDF trajectory to per-residue RMSD and partial decomposition analysis.
  • Ships with examples, docs, and scaffolding so it's reproducible by someone who didn't write it.

2026

Traffic sign classification with CNNs

Four convolutional architectures compared on 6,164 Chinese traffic sign images across 58 classes: a baseline CNN, an augmented version, and two MobileNetV2 transfer-learning variants. The best model ships as a command-line predictor with a Bash wrapper.

  • Both MobileNetV2 variants hit 98% test accuracy; the baseline CNN reached 96%.
  • Augmentation dropped accuracy to 68% — the baseline was barely overfitting, so it mostly added noise.
  • Confusion matrices showed errors spread thinly across classes rather than concentrated in confusable signs.

120 years of Olympic Games

A full pipeline from raw Kaggle data to published dashboards: 271,116 athlete-event records from 1896 to 2016 loaded into a Neon PostgreSQL database, queried into purpose-built tables, then visualized in interactive Tableau Public dashboards.

  • Four framing questions drove the queries: expansion, gender distribution, national medal counts, and first-five versus last-five Games.
  • Queries span window functions, CTEs, subqueries, and CASE statements — cumulative medal totals had to be constructed from scratch.
  • Dashboards use filters, parameters, and navigation buttons so a reader can move between questions in one workbook.

2026

Electrocatalyst performance prediction

Models electrocatalytic activity for the hydrogen evolution reaction on 545 experimental samples with 13 features. Random forests classify catalysts as good or bad; multilayer perceptrons predict overpotential directly. Two model families, same data, interrogated the same way.

  • Random forest reached a test F1 of 0.76 after tuning, up from a baseline scoring 1.00 on training and 0.69 on validation — textbook overfitting.
  • Plotting a representative tree showed leaves splitting on a handful of points, which drove the depth and leaf-size tuning.
  • MLP regression reached a test RMSE of 0.11, with errors consistent across all three splits.

Cardiovascular risk factor visualization

Exploratory analysis of patient medical data looking for relationships between physiological and behavioral features and cardiovascular disease. Feature engineering, outlier removal, and two complementary views: a categorical plot and a correlation heatmap.

  • After removing implausible records, no single feature correlated strongly — the strongest sat between 0.17 and 0.33.
  • Systolic blood pressure, cholesterol, age, and weight led; smoking, alcohol, and activity showed little to none.
  • The two views disagreed, and the write-up works out why rather than picking the flattering one.
